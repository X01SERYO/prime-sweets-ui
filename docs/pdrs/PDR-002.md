# PDR-002 — Current week and delivery day

**Status:** Proposal
**Language:** English
**Scope:** Next feature after Version 1
**Document role:** What the seller does in the web client. Not a description of the running UI.

The business source of truth is the Core API [PDR-002](../../../prime-sweets-api/docs/pdrs/PDR-002.md). This document does not redefine the week, the delivery date, Load Sheet aggregation, invoice numbers, or returns. Excel import and Alegra sync are API-only. This client does not gain screens for them.

## 1. Purpose

The seller works from the current week, records a delivery date on every sale, prepares the Load Sheet for a delivery day, and corrects or removes a sale when the store returns product on that day.

## 2. What the seller can do

1. Open the hub and see the Sales Days of the current week only. Days from the previous week are not listed.
2. Open a sale from an earlier day of this week and change it only while that Sales Day is open. A closed day must be reopened first.
3. After choosing the customer, answer when the order will be delivered before adding a product. The question appears for every new sale.
4. Change that date while the sale is still in the current week and its Sales Day is open.
5. Open the Load Sheet for a delivery date and read the products to load that day.
6. On the delivery day, open the sale, change quantities or lines, or remove the sale completely.
7. Print invoices as in Version 1. A voided sale stays listed and is not printable. A corrected sale keeps the number already on screen.

## 3. What the UI does not decide

The API decides whether the sale is inside the week, whether the delivery date is valid, which sales form the Load Sheet, and the totals. The client sends the date the seller picked and shows the result.

The client does not offer a return form, a negative quantity, or a new invoice number.

## 4. Screens

| Screen | When it is available | Primary actions |
|---|---|---|
| Week | Session exists | List this week's Sales Days. Open one day. |
| Sale | That Sales Day is in the current week, customer selected | Ask delivery date, then capture or correct lines, confirm, void |
| Delivery sales | A delivery date is selected | List sales for that date. Open one to correct or void it. |
| Load Sheet | A delivery date is selected | Read the sheet. No print. |

The customer step stays in front of the delivery-date question. Product search stays disabled until the date is set.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

- The hub does not show a Sales Day from the previous week.
- A new sale cannot add a line until a delivery date is on the sale.
- The seller can open Monday's sale on Wednesday of the same week and void it or change a quantity.
- The Load Sheet control asks for a delivery date, not for a closed Sales Day.
- Removing a sale uses the existing void action.
- Catalog import and Alegra are not in this client.
