# PDR-002 — Work by delivery day

**Status:** Proposal (revised 2026-11-01)
**Language:** English
**Scope:** Next feature after Version 1
**Document role:** What the seller does in the web client. Not a description of the running UI.

The business source of truth is the Core API [PDR-002](../../../prime-sweets-api/docs/pdrs/PDR-002.md), including its decisions and open questions. This document does not redefine allowed dates, Load Sheet aggregation, invoice numbers, or returns. Product administration is [PDR-003](./PDR-003.md).

## 1. Purpose

The seller sells at any time and organizes everything else by delivery day: what to load, what to print, and what to correct. There is no day to open or close.

## 2. What the seller can do

1. Start a new sale from the home screen at any time.
2. Choose the customer, then pick the delivery day (today through Sunday), before adding a product.
3. Pick a delivery day of the current week, or browse earlier weeks, and for that day:
   - see its sales, including drafts, and print the invoices from the same list;
   - read the Load Sheet;
   - open a sale to correct it (return, extra product, quantity, another delivery day) or remove it.
4. Correct or void a sale from any week. Moving a sale to another day only offers today through Sunday.
5. When a confirmed sale is moved to another day, the API voids its invoice on the old day and creates a new invoice on the new day. The client warns before doing it and then opens the new sale.

## 3. What the UI does not decide

The API decides which dates are allowed, which sales form the Load Sheet, invoice numbers, and totals. The client sends the seller's choice and shows the result.

## 4. Screens

| Screen | Primary actions |
|---|---|
| Home (delivery days) | New sale. Week of delivery days with today selected; previous/next week navigation. For the selected day: counts, Ventas, Cargue. |
| Sale | Customer → delivery day → lines, confirm, void, change delivery day. |
| Ventas of a delivery day | Drafts, confirmed, and voided. Open one to correct or void it. Select and print invoices. |
| Load Sheet | Read the sheet for the day. No print. |

Removed: the Sales Day list, open, close, and reopen.

## 5. Home screen

- The heading is the selected delivery day ("Domingo 1 de noviembre").
- **Nueva venta** is the main button, always available.
- A row with the seven days of the shown week, Monday to Sunday. Today is labeled "Hoy" and selected by default.
- **Previous week** and **next week** controls. Previous goes back without limit. Next stops at the current week.
- The selected day shows a summary (drafts, confirmed, voided, and total confirmed amount) and two direct actions: **Ventas** (list, correct, print) and **Cargue**.

## 6. Invoice print

The thermal invoice shows the confirmation date as the invoice date, and the delivery date under the label **"Fecha de entrega"** (formerly "Fecha de vencimiento").

## 7. Acceptance criteria

- No screen asks to open, close, or reopen anything.
- A new sale cannot add a line until a delivery day is on the sale.
- From one delivery day the seller reaches its sales (with printing) and its Load Sheet without choosing the day again.
- Moving a confirmed sale shows a warning, and afterwards the old day lists it as voided and the new day lists the new invoice.
- The seller can go back to an earlier week and correct a sale there.
- The next-week control is disabled on the current week.
- Product administration is [PDR-003](./PDR-003.md). This feature does not add it.

## 8. Decisions (2026-11-01)

| Id | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| UI-OQ-1 | Days on the home | The week Monday–Sunday, with previous/next week; next is capped at the current week. |
| UI-OQ-2 | Counts on the selected day | Yes: drafts, confirmed, voided, and total confirmed amount, from the sales list response. |
| UI-OQ-3 | Ventas and Facturas | One screen and one button, **Ventas**, with select and print. |
