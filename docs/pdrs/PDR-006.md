# PDR-006 — Pedidos screen

**Status:** Proposal
**Language:** English
**Scope:** A **Pedidos** section: suppliers, the products of a supplier, and a new purchase order sheet.
**Document role:** Product design requirement. Not a description of the running UI.

Business rules stay in the Core API [PDR-005](../../../prime-sweets-api/docs/pdrs/PDR-005.md). This document does not redefine cost, last quantity, or what is stored.

## 1. Purpose

The seller needs one place to say who they buy from and to write the next order. The sheet must show the last order's quantity beside a blank input, the cost of what they type, and a total, without opening older orders.

## 2. What the seller sees

1. **Pedidos** in the Spanish navigation, **Purchase orders** in English, next to the existing sections (sidebar on PC, bottom bar on phone). "Order" alone is not used: that word is the customer sale.
2. A supplier list: name and how many products are linked. Actions: new supplier, rename, open.
3. On a supplier: product search (same behavior as other product searches: results under the field, no modal). Linking adds the product. Each linked row can be removed from the supplier.
4. **Nuevo pedido** opens the sheet for that supplier.
5. Columns, in order: Código, Marca, Producto, Costo unitario, Cantidad anterior, Cantidad, Nota, Costo. Cantidad anterior is blank when there is no last order for that product. Cantidad is an empty integer input. Nota is an optional text input (flavor or other description). Costo is unit cost × the typed quantity, or blank when the quantity is empty.
6. A total under the table: sum of the row costs. It updates as the seller types. Nothing is saved until **Guardar**.
7. After save, a short confirmation and return to the supplier. A later **Nuevo pedido** shows the quantities just saved in Cantidad anterior.
8. A short list of previous orders for that supplier: date, number of lines, total. Opening one shows lines only when the API says this order still has them. Older rows explain that the detail is no longer kept.

## 3. Rules

- The sheet lists only products linked to the supplier. Search on this sheet filters that list; it does not add catalog products. Products are added on the supplier screen.
- The seller cannot edit Costo unitario or Cantidad anterior.
- Guardar sends only rows with a quantity of at least 1. Empty rows are left out.
- Labels follow the existing Spanish / English switch.
- Phone layout stacks the same fields; it does not drop a column's data. The PC shell rules in PDR-005 (fluid width) apply; this screen does not add a breakpoint.

## 4. Out of scope

- Stock, receiving, and editing a saved order.
- Printing the order.
- Changing catalog prices from this screen.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

- **Pedidos** is reachable from the main navigation on PC and on a phone width.
- Creating a supplier, linking a product found by code, and saving an order with a quantity and a note shows the line cost and the order total before save.
- Reloading and starting another order shows the saved quantity as Cantidad anterior and an empty Cantidad input.
- An order that is no longer the latest opens without a product table and still shows its total.
- Por mayor, Detal, Cargue, and Productos stay as they are.
